# Israel-linked startup funding and international R&D programs

**Verification date:** 2026-10-17  
**Scope:** Official Israeli public-sector programs, bilateral funds, BIRD programs, and international co-funded calls that can involve startups or technology companies across borders.

## Important conclusion

The verified sources did **not** establish a program that allows a non-Israeli startup to apply alone and receive direct Israeli public funding without an Israeli partner or a participating national/regional funder.

The verified routes fall into three practical classes:

1. **Bilateral programs requiring an Israeli partner:** the foreign company applies through its own national funder while the Israeli company applies to Israel Innovation Authority (IIA), BIRD, or the relevant bilateral body.
2. **Multilateral programs requiring an Israeli participant:** the foreign startup joins an international consortium and follows its own country’s funding rules; the Israeli participant uses the Israeli route.
3. **Foreign startup alone:** no confirmed route was found in this review.

## What a foreign startup should prepare

- A qualifying Israeli company or industrial R&D partner.
- A foreign-side national or regional funder route, where required.
- A signed LOI or consortium MoU covering work split, IP ownership, commercialization rights, access to results, budget, and responsibilities.
- Incorporation certificates, ownership structure, KYC, financial statements, matching-funds evidence, and company capabilities.
- Technical proposal, innovation case, target market, commercialization plan, work packages, milestones, TRL, and country-by-country budget.
- BIRD Executive Summary before a full proposal, when applying to BIRD.
- Parallel submissions to the Israeli and foreign funding systems by the applicable deadline.

Do not describe these programs as unrestricted grants or equity investment. Several are conditional grants, cost-sharing mechanisms, or royalty-linked support. The applicable call controls when a standing program page and a specific call show different amounts or deadlines.
